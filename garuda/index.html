How the fight runs

1. Ground: Garuda chases Durga, fires wind gusts and does low-flying lunges. Hit him and ring bells to empty a segment. Curse refills slowly up to the current divider. A gust that crosses a fan with a tied rope helps hoist that bell.
2. Flight (after segments 1, 2, 3): Garuda flies, can't be hit, does nothing. Take a fan's rope and tie it to its fallen bell.
3. Sleep (after segment 4): ring bells to add feed time, hold F next to him to feed. Fail = segment 4 refills, back to ground.

Keys

WASD move, Shift sprint, Space dodge (gusts and lunges can't hit you mid-dodge)
E does whatever the prompt under Durga says: ring bell / take rope / tie rope
H stick hit, F hold to feed, G debug: break current segment
Hover any slider to see what it changes. Drag Garuda, Durga, bells or fans with the mouse.
