Controls

All distances are Unreal units (uu), speeds are uu/s.
WASD / arrows = move Durga. Shift = sprint. Space = dodge.
E = strike the bell you are standing at (Durga is locked during the strike).
Hold F = feed leaf (next to a dazed Garuda; Durga can't move while feeding; progress is kept if she leaves).
H = stick hit (next to Garuda, not dazed). G = force daze.
Drag Garuda, Durga or any bell with the mouse. 1/2/3 = debug strike from anywhere.
